Weights come from home-mixer/params/param.rs and the multipliers from vm-ranker/params.rs in xai-org/x-algorithm (commit 9081b33, November 2026). In the real system the inputs are the model's predictions for one specific viewer, not counts, and it also uses dwell time, clicks into photos and quoted posts, and a few other signals left out here. The mutual follow boost adds 15 to the reply weight for original posts from accounts you follow that also follow you. Posts from accounts the viewer doesn't follow are multiplied by 0.75. Each extra post from the same author in one feed is multiplied by 0.5, down to 0.25. This is a teaching tool. It can't predict the reach of any real post.
